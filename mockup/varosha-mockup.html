How it works

A hand up, then a way forward.

Our mission is to give people a hand up. With the help of our partners, we run training centers to equip individuals with the skills they need to thrive.

1

Train

Beautician work, tailoring, jewelry making, mushroom cultivation, healthcare assistance, computers and spoken English. Courses run three to six months at partner centers in Kolkata, Bolpur and Birbhum.

2

Lend

Once training is complete, Varosha provides interest-free loans to trainees so they can buy a sewing machine, stock a parlor, or set up a stall.

3

Thrive

Trainees start their own businesses and become self-sufficient. Many come back to recommend the course to the next group.

Our Story

A simple idea, held for twenty years.

Everyone should have a chance to provide a decent quality of life for themselves and their families.

Varosha began in April 2004 with the simple idea that everyone should have a chance to provide a decent quality of life for themselves and their families. As many around the world do not have the means or opportunity to learn employable skills, they are stuck within a cycle of poverty and hopelessness. Varosha is a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing education, resources, and expertise to economically empower individuals.

Throughout the years, we have supported a range of training programs in various fields such as beautician training, tailoring, Jori work, pasta making, embroidery, mushroom cultivation, agricultural assistance, fish farming and small animal husbandry, computer training, and healthcare assistance.

Our mission is to give people a hand up, and with the help of our partners, we run training centers to equip individuals with the skills they need to thrive. Once the training is complete, Varosha provides interest-free loans to trainees, enabling them to start their own businesses and become self-sufficient.

Our mission

Where we work

Kolkata (Chetla, New Alipore, Tangra Dhapa, Ulta Danga, Topsia), Bolpur, Birbhum, Mallarpur, and the Sunderbans (Sonakhali, Khulna), West Bengal.

Our partners

Hope (Chetla Skills Unit), HVR (Aranyer Alo), Rotary Club of Calcutta Inner City and Pub Paschim, and the Dipto Alo and Jibon Alo schools.

How we are run

A registered 501(c)(3) led by a volunteer board in the Chicago area. All administrative expenses are donated by directors and volunteers.

    Scholarship

    Ananda Chakrabarty Memorial Scholarship

    Varosha offers one scholarship a year to a low-income Illinois student looking for financial support to attend college or university.

    The scholarship is for $5,000 per year, with the opportunity to renew annually, for a total of four years of support, based on the student continuing to meet the required metrics.

    Eligibility

    Students must take either the SAT or ACT and maintain a B average to be eligible for and maintain the scholarship.

    Include with your application

    • School transcripts
    • ACT or SAT score documentation
    • College or university admission letter
    • Three recommendation letters from teachers, employers, research advisors, or similar
    Applications for the fall 2026 school year are being accepted now and are due March 15, 2026.

    What we collect

    Varosha collects only the information you give us directly: your name and email when you write to us, the details on a scholarship application submitted through our Google Form, and the name and email on a donation so that we can send a tax receipt.
