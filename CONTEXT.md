## 1. The organization

- **Name:** Varosha (Bengali: "trust / hope")
- **Tagline (used everywhere):** "Hope and Empowerment through Education and Opportunity"
- **Founded:** April 2004
- **Legal:** Registered 501(c)(3). Donations tax-deductible. **100% of donations go to project expenses**; all admin costs are donated by directors/volunteers. (Strong claim, should be a hero-level trust signal.)
- **Where the work happens:** West Bengal, India — Kolkata (Chetla, New Alipore, Tangra Dhapa, Ulta Danga, Topsia), Bolpur, Birbhum, Mallarpur, Sunderbans (Sonakhali, Khulna)
- **Where the org is:** 1157 E Patten Dr, Palatine, IL 60074
- **Partners named:** Hope (Chetla Skills Unit), HVR (Aranyer Alo farming), Rotary Club of Calcutta Inner City / Pub Paschim
- **Contact:** contact@varosha.org · 847-920-8153 · facebook.com/varoshaorg
- **Donate:** Zelle donate@varosha.org (preferred, no fees) · Venmo @varosha · checks by mail
- **Scholarship:** Ananda Chakrabarty Memorial Scholarship — $5,000/yr, renewable 4 yrs, low-income Illinois student, SAT/ACT + B average, Google Form application

### What Varosha actually does (the model)
1. Fund a training program (tailoring, beautician, jewelry, mushroom farming, healthcare assistant, computers/English/Tally accounting, fine arts for kids, after-school tuition, meals).
2. On completion, give **interest-free micro-loans** so trainees start their own business.
3. Result: self-sufficiency. This "train → loan → independence" loop is the story the new site should tell.

### `/projects` Projects
- H1: Projects. Intro: "Here you'll find a selection of projects supported by Varosha over the years. Select a picture below to learn more about each project and what we do."
- Wix Pro Gallery, 11 items (title :: description :: image id):
  1. **Women's Jewelry Making Class** — Over 100 trainees in three centers: Tangra Dhapa, Ulta Danga Canal-side and Topsia Canal-side were trained in jewelry making classes for women and girls. Training included quilling, German silver, sea shells, bead making with silk and cotton thread.
  2. **Breakfast for 190 School Children** — School children in Mallarpur Center were provided with: puffed rice, milk and sugar (4 days), bread, sweet and banana (1 day), egg and bread (1 day) each week. Children in Kolkata Centers were given: fruits (2 days), egg (2 days) and health drinks (1 day) per week.
  3. **Mushroom Cultivation** — 9 young men and 7 young women from Birbhum, West Bengal, who were unemployed have completed the mushroom cultivation and marketing training project funded by Varosha. The 6 month course trained them to grow mushrooms (including spawn preparation) and also how to prepare and market preserved mushroom products which are rapidly gaining popularity. The trainees were so happy with their training that they recommended the class and we supported a second session.
  4. **Beautician Training** — Classes were held in Bolpur, West Bengal, and two centers in Kolkata with 52 young women are taking the classes. The curriculum covers: manicure, pedicure, waxing, eye brows, make up, facial, hair styling and saree draping. Additionally, they receive further training at a local beauty parlor.
  5. **Tailoring Program** — Varosha continues to support the Business Center for women who have completed the tailoring program and provide microloans to the trainees to assist them to buy sewing machines and supplies to start their businesses.
  6. **Chetla Skills Center** — From April 2017, with our partner Hope, we fully funded the operating expenses of the Skills Unit in Chetla. The project provides high quality vocational training in spoken English, computer skills, and financial accounting classes using Tally ERP-9. In 2019, 52 trainees from Hope Skill Unit received their certification and medals. Trophies were given to the 3 best performers in Basic Computer, English and Tally Financial course. Varosha is proud to financially support this center and help the students with job placement once they are trained.
  7. **Support for Farmers (Aranyer Alo)** — This project is in the villages of Sonakhali and Khulna in the Sunderban, South 24 Pargana West Bengal. Fallow land and marshes that were not suitable for proper farming were converted by local villagers for fish farming and selective agriculture thanks to our partnership with HVR. Several plots have been converted to fish farms. New plans and initiatives are in place to convert unused and unproductive lands to be productive. We have distributed seeds and small plants. The raising of small animals: chicken and goats, have been very financially successful for our beneficiaries.
  8. **After-School Tuition Centers** — One of our greatest joys is visiting the children's after school program in Chetla/New Alipore Basti. Over the years Varosha has supported the children by providing a healthy meal and a safe space after school. We have provided tuition to help them in school as well as spoken English, dance and art classes. Several students appeared for and passed the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education's Madhyamik Exam. Varosha's after school tuition centers is their only academic support.
  9. **Eye Health Camp** — Organized by Varosha with Rotary Club of Calcutta, Inner City and Pub Paschim on January 27, 2018 at Dipto Alo school in New Alipore, Kolkata. Free eye checkups were provided to 53 women and children. All the participants received snack packs. 23 children were diagnosed as needing eyeglasses. The prescription glasses have been ordered and will be distributed soon. Two ladies need cataract surgery and scheduled follow up visits with the doctor. Varosha will be funding these expenses.
  10. **Health-care Assistant Training** — This project prepares and train individuals from disadvantaged community to deliver general healthcare support to newborns, the elderly, sick and those recovering from surgeries. The training covers basic physiology, anatomy, hygiene, general skills like measuring blood pressure, temperature, dressing, administering medications, and physiotherapy. Candidates have a minimum of 8th grade education to be accepted into the program. They go through a 3 month education program and then are sent to a nursing home for 3 months of practical training. Once trained they are quickly finding work.
  11. **Fine Arts Academy - Jibon Alo** — Jibon Alo is a Children Academy in New Alipore slum, Kolkata, W Bengal. This academy teaches performing arts like: singing, dancing, acting, and creative arts like: painting, drawing, craft-work to disadvantaged children in the area. We believe that exposure to these creative arts will help the children as they grow. It has been inspiring to see the children discover their hidden talents and compete with children from affluent communities in festivals and competitions and win awards and accolades.

## 4. Brand direction (for the rebuild)

**Who visits:** Chicago-area Bengali-American donors (many already know the founders), Rotary/partner orgs, Illinois students applying for the scholarship, occasional grant reviewers.
**What they need in 5 seconds:** what Varosha does, proof it's real and long-running, and how to give.

**Voice:** warm, direct, plain-spoken, proud of specifics (52 trainees, 190 breakfasts, 16 mushroom farmers). Not corporate, not pity-driven.

**Visual anchors from the photos:** saturated textiles (magenta, vermilion, marigold yellow), henna, jewelry, Durga Puja pandal gold/blue, bright classroom walls. The UI should be calm and neutral so the photos carry the color.

**Aesthetic options presented 2026-09-25** (Prameet to research and choose):
1. Editorial / magazine (charity: water, Acumen, Malala Fund) — big photos, serif headlines, long-form storytelling
2. Warm humanist / craft (Nest, Anchal Project, Patagonia Action Works) — cream paper tones, earthy accent, textile-inspired details
3. Swiss / grid modernism (Gates Foundation, Ford Foundation, Wellcome) — strict grid, sans-serif, data-forward, restrained
4. Contemporary minimal with bold accent (GiveDirectly, Watsi) — lots of white, one strong accent, product-like clarity, conversion-focused

**Recommended:** 1 + 2 blend ("warm editorial").

**DECIDED 2026-09-25 — Palette: Option A "Lamplight"** (Prameet's choice)
- Paper `#FAF6EF` · Ink `#1F1B17` · Copper `#B8672E` (primary, from logo) · Flame `#E9A63A` (highlight, sparingly) · Night `#1E2A3A` (dark sections/footer) · Stone `#8A8078` (muted)
- No black backgrounds. Photos carry the saturation; UI stays warm-neutral.

**Earlier alternative (rejected), kept for reference:**
- Paper `#F8F4EC` (warm off-white background)
- Ink `#1E1B18` (near-black text)
- Vermilion `#C8412B` (primary accent — sindoor / textile red; CTAs, links)
- Marigold `#E8A62E` (secondary accent, sparingly — highlights, hover)
- Indigo `#23405F` (deep cool contrast — footers, dark sections, headings on light)
- Stone `#8C857B` (muted text, borders)
Alt (if Prameet wants cooler/more institutional): swap Vermilion for Teal `#146C6A` and keep Marigold.

**Type proposed:** one serif for display (e.g. Fraunces, Source Serif, or Newsreader) + one humanist sans for body/UI (e.g. Inter, Source Sans, or Figtree). Two families max.
